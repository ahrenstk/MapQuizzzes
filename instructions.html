Instructions

Welcome to the quiz!

Discover places from a completely new perspective! Grab your smartphone, select a tour, and head out to start your scavenger hunt.

How it works:

  • Installation (optional): On Android (Chrome) via the 3-dot menu select "Install", and on iOS (Safari) via the share button select "Add to Home Screen". You can uninstall the WebApp at any time.
  • No registration, no waiting: Get started immediately without an account – solve the riddles on-site, compare your answers directly with the solutions, and receive exciting background information as well!
  • Select a tour: Click on a yellow button.
  • Location & Navigation: Enable your location using the location icon in the top right corner of the map or tap directly on a marker to get navigation to the destination.
  • Riddles: Click on the images on the map for questions, valuable tips, and solutions.
  • Tracking: Solved riddles are color-coded on the map – this way, you can keep track of your progress during the tour.
  • Your own pace: Take as much time as you like. You can interrupt the tour for a break at any time and continue later at your convenience.

Good luck! If you enjoy the quiz, feel free to recommend it to others.
